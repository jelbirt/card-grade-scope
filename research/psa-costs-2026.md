## 2. The Value-tier pause (PRIMARY-LIVE)

Source: <https://www.psacard.com/articles/articleview/15210/service-level-update-may-2026> ("Due to Demand Spike, PSA Pauses Value Tiers, Extends Collectors Club Memberships, and Launches Public Backlog Tracker"), accessed 2026-08-01.

- Effective **2026-06-02**, new submissions paused for **Value Bulk, Value, Value Plus, Value Max**.
- Regular, Express, Super Express, Walk-Through remain open. Regular turnaround extended to 40–50 business days.
- Backlog was "rapidly approaching 10 million" cards; reinstatement is tied to reaching a **5 million** backlog, projected "up to four months" (i.e., roughly October 2026, not guaranteed). PSA publishes a monthly Backlog Tracker on that page.
- Collectors Club memberships active on 2026-05-14 are **extended free** for the duration of the Value Bulk pause.

**Implication for this tool:** grading cost must be modeled per *pricing scenario* — "current (Regular is cheapest at $79.99)" vs. "Value tiers restored" — because the cheapest orderable price per card differs by 3.2× and flips most verdicts for a modest childhood collection.

## 5. Shipping to PSA (PRIMARY-LIVE + SECONDARY for costs)

Source: <https://www.psacard.com/info/shipguide> ("PSA Shipping Guide"), accessed 2026-08-01.

Requirements (primary):
- Each card in a **clear penny sleeve** (no opaque sleeves), inside a **semi-rigid holder** (Card Saver I-style; 3 5/16" × 4 7/8" for standard cards). **No toploaders**, tape, pull tabs, or sticky notes.
- Cards stacked in submission-form order between **two cardboard dividers**, secured with 2–3 rubber bands; bubble-wrapped in a **new** box; all seams taped with packing tape.
- Printed submission form inside the box; tracking label ID from confirmation page on the outside.
- Ship to: USPS → CU Receiving, P.O. Box 6180, Newport Beach, CA 92658; UPS/FedEx/DHL → CU Receiving, 1600 E. St Andrew Place, Santa Ana, CA 92705.

Inbound cost (SECONDARY, varies by carrier/value): typical $12–$30 for a small tracked-and-insured package (e.g., USPS Priority + third-party insurance ~$1–3 per $100 declared). Sources: <https://figoca.com/blog/psa/how-to-submit-to-psa-step-by-step-guide-2026>, <https://www.secursus.com/en-us/resource/article/how-to-ship-trading-cards/>, accessed 2026-08-01. **Model as a configurable line item, default $25, marked estimate.**

## 7. Upcharges (SECONDARY, consistent across sources)

If PSA judges a card's post-grading market value to exceed the selected tier's max insured value, the card is moved to the appropriate tier and the difference charged. Sources: <https://slabd.com.au/psa-upcharges/>, <https://powerssportsmemorabilia.com/blogs/news/psa-upcharges-explained-and-how-graded-value-is-calculated>, accessed 2026-08-01. PSA's own T&C page governs; exact trigger thresholds are not published — **UNVERIFIED**. The tool should *warn* when a card's PSA-10 value exceeds its chosen tier's max insured value (upcharge risk), not attempt to price it.
